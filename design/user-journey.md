# User Journey (Alpha) 

The Overseer, Jim, creates a task entry for emptying sinks and dish racks every week on Sunday due between 12:00 AM and 11:59 PM on Sunday with the weighting of a light chore. The criteria requires both sinks and dish racks to be free of dishes. Jim uploads a reference photo and types instructions of what should be visible in a photo of the completed chore. The standard is fixed for this job.

Residents enter their availability and preferences. On Saturday night, chores for the coming week are scheduled automatically. As Alice is available on weekends and prefers kitchen chores, she is assigned this chore by the system. Alice sees the task in her queue and does it.

Overseer opens the queue and sees a new submission by Alice and is awaiting review. The job description appears with the photo that Alice submitted. The Overseer compares the photo to the description on one screen. With AI integration enabled, the role of the Overseer in this paragraph is replaced by an agent, but the agent has an additional option to respond “uncertain” which sends the submission directly to the Overseer for manual approval.

The Overseer sees that photo cuts off between the sink and the dish rack and therefore does not show whether the full job was completed, so the Overseer rejects the submission and types a note on why. 

Later, the Overseer sees that Alice has resubmitted to the queue. The Overseer views the photo and criteria and sees that the job is properly completed. They mark the submission approved and the task is updated to be completed.
